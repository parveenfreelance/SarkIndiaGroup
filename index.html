Why it matters

Why Earthing & Lightning Protection is Important?

A single strike or fault can cost lives, equipment and years of work. Proper protection safely carries that energy into the ground.

Protection of Life

Reduces the risk of electric shock and lightning-related injuries for occupants and maintenance personnel.

Prevention of Fire Hazards

Safely dissipates fault currents and lightning energy to prevent electrical fires from overheating, short circuits or strikes.

Safeguarding Equipment

Protects electrical and electronic equipment from voltage surges — reducing damage, downtime and costly repairs.

Structural & Property Safety

Prevents direct lightning impact on buildings, minimizing structural damage and maintaining property integrity.

Compliance & Reliability

Ensures compliance with safety standards and improves electrical system stability and long-term performance.

Featured Product

SARK Smart Lightning Arrester (ESE)

The ASLA range of Early Streamer Emission (ESE) Lightning Arresters offers a safe and reliable system to protect your building or solar project from direct lightning strikes. Also known as Active Lightning Arresters, they provide an enhanced radius of protection — reducing the number of arresters and down conductors needed while still providing complete protection.

Enhanced radius of protection
Fewer arresters & down conductors
Ideal for buildings & solar projects
Models: ASLA-15, ASLA-30, ASLA-60
The protection radius depends on height (h) above the reference plane, arrester efficiency and protection level: Rp(h) = √(2rh − h² + Δ(2r + Δ)) for h ≥ 5 m Rp = h × Rp(5) / 5 for 2 m ≤ h ≤ 5 m r = 20 / 30 / 45 / 60 m for Protection Level I / II / III / IV · Δ = 20 / 30 / 63 m for ASLA-15 / 30 / 60
